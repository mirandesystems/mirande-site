Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

Yes. You build routines on iPhone (iOS 17.6 or later) and run them on an Apple Watch (watchOS 10.6 or later). Without a Watch, routines can't be run.

Will it tap me with my wrist down?

Yes. While a routine runs, Anchored stays active for up to an hour so taps come through with the screen off. If you press the Digital Crown or switch apps, the remaining step changes arrive as notifications instead.

Can it start when I put my watch on?

watchOS doesn't let any app know when you put your watch on. Instead, a reminder rings at your time and opens straight to your routine — one tap on Start.

What happens to my data?

It stays on your iPhone and Watch. It moves with your iPhone backup, and deleting the app deletes it from your devices. Read the privacy policy.

Is it really one payment?

Yes. One in-app purchase unlocks unlimited routines and history for good, on every iPhone signed in to your Apple Account. With Family Sharing, your family gets it too.
